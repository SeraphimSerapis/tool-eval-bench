**Truncated tool-choice probes retry before excluding TC-45.** A probe that reaches its 256-token limit without a tool call now retries once at 4096 tokens, so reasoning that runs past the initial limit is no longer mistaken for an endpoint ignoring `tool_choice="required"`. Repeated truncation is reported as inconclusive and still excludes the scenario from scoring. Diagnostics include each attempt's finish reason, completion-token count, and retry outcome. The probe's budgets are independent of `max_tokens`, `max_completion_tokens`, and Gemini `maxOutputTokens` backend kwargs, including `maxOutputTokens` nested in `generationConfig`; scenario budgets and scoring rules are unchanged.
