**Plain console output no longer pins verdicts on the wrong scenario under `--parallel`.** With `--no-live` and `--parallel N`, the console printed each start line without a newline and appended verdicts in completion order, so a verdict landed next to whichever title was printed last, and the final few appeared with no scenario at all. Parallel plain mode now prints one complete line per finished scenario (`[done/total] TC-xx Title... verdict`) in completion order. Sequential output is unchanged. Scores, stored results and reports were always correct; only the console was wrong.
