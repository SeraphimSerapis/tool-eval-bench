**TC-50 evaluates each assistant message individually before the earliest send_email turn.**
`asked_who` previously joined every recorded message, so an ask that appeared after the email, a
negated statement ("I don't need to ask who the PM is"), a quoted mention, a meta mention, or
phrase fragments split across turns could earn credit, and a contact lookup made after the email
was credited as the grounding lookup. The ask is now detected per message with the negated, quoted,
and meta mentions rejected, only up to the earliest send_email turn, and the credited contact lookup
must precede the email. Sending without any ask still gets PARTIAL, and sending before the user
reveals the recipient (`user_phase < 1`) still FAILs.
