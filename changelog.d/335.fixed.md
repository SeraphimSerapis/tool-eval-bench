**TC-45 probe and exclusion reporting.** The required-tool probe no longer contradicts its own wire constraint or excludes scenarios when it fails. TC-45 runs its own request, with probe results retained as diagnostics rather than proof of server-side enforcement. `--no-probe-engine` and the Python API's `probe_engine=False` now skip this probe. Plain terminal summaries also name excluded scenario IDs; JSON continues to record them under `scores.excluded_scenarios`.
