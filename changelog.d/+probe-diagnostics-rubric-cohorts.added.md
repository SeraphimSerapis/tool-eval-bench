**Chat-template probes, loop and withheld-score diagnostics, and rubric-versioned cohorts:**
- **Chat-template replay probes and Gemma 4 parser markers (`probe --check-template`):** Added `runner/template_probe.py` with paired tool-replay and multi-turn fidelity checks (`tool_replay`/`tool_replay_twin`, `multi_turn`/`multi_turn_twin`) to separate server Jinja template defects from model capability. Added stdlib `extract_special_tokens()` in `utils/tokenizers.py` and `detect_special_token_leak()` plus Gemma 4 tool/channel markers (`<|tool_call>`, `<channel|>`, `<turn|>`, `call:<name>{...}`) in `domain/wire_hygiene.py`. Exposed `--check-template` and `--tokenizer` on `probe` (with `--json` `template_probe` events) and updated `SCHEMA_VERSION` to `13`.
- **Withheld scores, degenerate-loop detection, and `answer_in_thinking` diagnostics:** `score_results()` now returns `final_score=None` and `rating="Withheld"` (with `weighted_score=None` and `deployability=None`) whenever `max_points == 0` instead of reporting `0`, and CLI, reports, cards, and the web UI render withheld runs cleanly. Added stdlib-only `domain/loop_detection.py` (`FailureKind.DEGENERATE_LOOP`) to catch repeated 20-gram word cycles and degenerate character/whitespace runs separately from genuine `REASONING_TRUNCATED` cutoffs, and tracked clean reasoning-only empty turns (`answer_in_thinking_turns`, `answer_in_thinking_scenarios`, `degenerate_loop_scenarios`).
- **Rubric-versioned leaderboard cohorts and comparability warnings:** Added `domain/rubric.py` (`EVAL_RUBRIC_VERSION`, `LOCALE_VERSION`, `NOISE_VERSION`, and a docstring-stripped cross-version AST fingerprint gate over `evals/` and `runner/orchestrator.py`), recorded rubric versions on `RunContext`, decoupled `cohort_fingerprint()` from unrelated `git_sha`/`tool_version` commits (keeping legacy fallback for pre-rubric stored runs), excluded withheld runs from leaderboard eligibility, and added `comparability_warnings()` to leaderboard rows and paired run comparisons.
